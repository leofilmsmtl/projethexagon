Projet Hexagon

Delete your account

Projet Hexagon · 9574-9677 Québec Inc. · version of November 24, 2026

This is a translation. The French version prevails.

1. In the game

In the Projet Hexagon app:

  1. 1Tap the gear at the bottom of the screen.
  2. 2Tap Account.
  3. 3Tap Delete my account.
  4. 4Confirm with your password. An account opened with Google or Apple, without a password, gets a code at its email instead.

2. Without the game

Write to contact@projethexagon.com from your account’s email, with the subject “Delete my account” and your username. A 6-digit code is then sent to your account’s email; send it back in a reply, within 7 days, to confirm the account is yours. The account is then deleted as in the game (section 3), and an email gives you the erasure date and a recovery code. The request is handled within 30 days.

3. What happens

Right awayYour account disappears from other players’ map and leaderboard, and your session ends.
For 30 daysYour account can be restored: just sign in again.
After 30 daysYour account is erased, with everything in it. The link with your Apple account, if any, is also removed at Apple.

4. What is erased, what remains

Erased: your email, your username, your password, your Google and Apple sign-ins, your game (discoveries, inventory, gems, drawings, progress), your consents, your playtime and your positions.

Remains: your hexes stay on the map, without your name. Encrypted backups keep your account until they expire, 6 months at most; they are used only to repair a failure.

5. Start over without deleting your account

To erase your game and keep your account, in the Projet Hexagon app:

  1. 1Tap the gear at the bottom of the screen.
  2. 2Tap Account.
  3. 3Tap Start over.
  4. 4Confirm with your password, or with the code sent to your email.

Erased: your discoveries and your map, your drawings, your gems, your badges and your progress. Your hexes become free for everyone.

Remains: your account, your sign-in, your name and your color. Your GPS points erase themselves after 7 days, as always.

This is final: what is erased cannot be recovered.
