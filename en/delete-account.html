Delete your account

Projet Hexagon · 9574-9677 Québec Inc. · version of November 24, 2026

This is a translation. The French version prevails.

1. In the game

In the Projet Hexagon app:

  1. Tap the gear at the bottom of the screen.
  2. Tap “Account”.
  3. Tap “Delete my account”.
  4. Confirm with your password. An account opened with Google or Apple, without a password, gets a code at its email instead.

2. Without the game

Write to contact@projethexagon.com from your account’s email, with the subject “Delete my account” and your username. A code may be sent to that email to confirm the account is yours. The request is handled within 30 days.

3. What happens

WhenWhat
Right awayYour account disappears from other players’ map and leaderboard, and your session ends.
For 30 daysYour account can be restored: just sign in again.
After 30 daysYour account is erased, with everything in it. The link with your Apple account, if any, is also removed at Apple.

4. What is erased, what remains

Erased: your email, your username, your password, your Google and Apple sign-ins, your game (discoveries, inventory, gems, drawings, progress), your consents, your playtime and your positions.

Remains: your hexes stay on the map, without your name. Encrypted backups keep your account until they expire, 6 months at most; they are used only to repair a failure.
